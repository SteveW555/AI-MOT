## Common Questions & Answers

### General AI Questions

**Q: What is AI and how can it help my small business?**
A: AI (Artificial Intelligence) refers to computer systems that can perform tasks that typically require human intelligence - like understanding language, recognizing patterns, and making decisions. For small businesses, AI can automate repetitive tasks (saving time), provide insights from data (better decisions), improve customer experiences (24/7 support), and help you compete with larger companies (level playing field). Think of AI as a tireless, data-savvy assistant that works 24/7 to make your business more efficient and effective.

**Q: Is AI only for tech companies?**
A: Not at all! AI is being successfully used by businesses in every industry - from retail stores using AI for inventory management, to dental offices using AI for appointment scheduling, to construction companies using AI for project estimation. If your business has data, customers, or repetitive processes, there's likely an AI application that can help.

**Q: How much does AI cost?**
A: AI costs vary widely depending on the solution. Some AI tools like ChatGPT cost $20/month per user. Custom AI implementations for small businesses typically range from $8,000 to $25,000. However, the ROI often justifies the investment - many of our clients see 300-500% ROI within 12 months through time savings, increased sales, or reduced costs. Our Consultation service ($2,500) includes a detailed ROI projection for your specific use cases.

**Q: Do I need technical expertise to use AI?**
A: No! Modern AI tools are designed for non-technical users. You don't need to know how to code or understand complex algorithms. Our training programs teach you to use AI tools effectively with practical, hands-on exercises. We focus on prompt engineering and practical application, not technical jargon.

**Q: What's the difference between AI and automation?**
A: Automation follows pre-programmed rules (if this happens, do that). AI learns from data and can handle situations it hasn't seen before. For example: automation can send a follow-up email 3 days after purchase; AI can read customer replies, understand the sentiment, and route urgent issues to human support. AI is "smart automation" that adapts and learns.

However, this is rare - our pre-implementation Consultation ensures we only pursue high-probability-of-success projects.

### Data & Privacy Questions

**Q: Is my business data safe with AI?**
A: Data security is our top priority. We follow enterprise-grade security practices: encrypted data transmission, secure cloud storage, access controls, and compliance with regulations (GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA where applicable). We also help you choose AI vendors with strong security track records. Every project includes a Security & Compliance review.

**Q: Who owns the AI and the data?**
A: You do! Any custom AI systems we build are 100% owned by you (we provide full source code and documentation). Your business data remains your property - we only access it to build/train your AI systems, and we delete or return it per your preference after project completion.

**Q: Can AI accidentally leak confidential information?**
A: Properly configured AI systems have safeguards against data leakage. We implement "data access controls" (AI only sees what it needs), "output filtering" (preventing sensitive data in responses), and "audit logs" (tracking what data was accessed). Our Audit service specifically checks for these risks in existing AI systems.

### Technical Questions

**Q: What's the difference between GPT-4 and other AI models?**
A: GPT-4 (OpenAI) is one of many Large Language Models. Others include Claude (Anthropic), Gemini (Google), and Llama (Meta). Each has strengths:
- GPT-4: Best general-purpose model, strong reasoning
- Claude: Best for long documents, nuanced analysis
- Gemini: Best for Google Workspace integration
- Llama: Best for on-premise, privacy-focused use cases

We help you choose the right model for your use case in our Consultation.

**Q: Can AI integrate with my existing software (CRM, ERP, etc.)?**
A: Yes! Most modern business software has APIs that allow AI integration. We've successfully integrated AI with:
- CRMs: Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho
- ERPs: NetSuite, SAP, QuickBooks, Xero
- Marketing: Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, Marketo
- Custom software: We build API connections as needed

Our Implementation service includes full integration with your tech stack.

**Q: Do you build custom AI or use off-the-shelf tools?**
A: Both! We follow a "buy vs. build" framework:
- **Off-the-shelf**: When proven tools exist (80% of use cases) - faster, cheaper, lower risk
- **Custom build**: When your needs are unique or you want competitive advantage (20%) - tailored, proprietary, higher impact

We recommend the most cost-effective approach in our Consultation.

3. Provide examples of how AI handles repetitive tasks so humans can focus on high-value work
4. Mention our Training service helps teams embrace AI as a productivity tool
5. Emphasize that businesses using AI create more jobs, not fewer (need AI managers, strategists, etc.)
